04 / About the numbers

Transparent, not a timesheet.

The headline is an owner-reported estimate of personal effort, recorded on 8 October 2026. It is not an independently audited or automatically tracked total.

What the estimate covers

Planning, design decisions, content and source work, development direction, hands-on review, testing, launch preparation and feedback. AI runtime and GitHub activity alone cannot establish how long a person was actively working.

How the breakdown was prepared

The six categories are an illustrative allocation of the total, not six measured time logs. A work session can involve several activities; the display assigns the overall estimate once rather than adding overlapping sessions.

How the figures are maintained

This is a dated snapshot, not a live counter. Future revisions require the project owner to update the estimate and its date. The page does not track your visits, create a timer, or collect new personal information.
