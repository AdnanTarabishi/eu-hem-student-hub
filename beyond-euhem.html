How the research will work

Useful detail, a clear evidence trail and respect for the people behind each journey.

  1. Start with a reliable source.Use university-published interviews and voluntary contributions. Record the source, its date and when the information was checked.
  2. Verify the study context.Confirm cohort and track where possible. Historical track names will remain attached to their own cohort rather than being relabelled as current tracks.
  3. Separate facts from gaps.Include thesis direction, first role and later moves only where supported. Missing information stays unknown; a role will not be inferred from a name or degree.
  4. Review before publication.Original profiles and contributed stories require graduate approval and editorial review. Published summaries will retain source links and a route for corrections or removal.
