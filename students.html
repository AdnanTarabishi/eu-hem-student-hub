        Source: supplied “Countries where you come from” overview · 103+2 pax. About these numbers

        The 24 country counts add up to 105. The source does not explain the distinction between the 103 and the additional 2 people; this page uses the full listed total and preserves the original notation.

        Every percentage uses 105 as its denominator, including when you search or filter the country list. Percentages are rounded to one decimal place and may not add up to exactly 100%.
