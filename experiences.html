A few things to know

Real perspectives.
Clear context.

Student stories complement official information; they do not replace it.

Where do the current stories come from?

The launch collection contains editorial summaries of interviews and testimonials already published by MCI Innsbruck and Erasmus University Rotterdam. Every overview links to the original university page.

Can current students contribute?

Yes. The form is designed so a second-year student can share the semesters and cities already experienced without pretending the whole degree is complete. Completed, in-progress and planned stages remain separate.

Are the images photographs of the students?

Not unless a story explicitly says so. The current covers are editorial illustrations chosen to reflect the city or topic of each story. They are labeled as AI-generated and should not be interpreted as portraits of the contributor. Official university photography can replace an illustration later when reuse permission is clear.

Can someone publish a story without publishing their profile?

Yes. A future Student Experiences submission and a Student Directory profile are separate. A contributor can choose the audience and identity display for the story independently.

Does “no name” guarantee anonymity?

No. In a small international cohort, a very specific route or background can still identify someone. The preview therefore lets contributors hide optional cohort and background details too.

Why is the Submit button disabled?

The public interface is ready for drafting and preview, but real submission will stay closed until a private intake, editorial review, author approval and withdrawal workflow is configured and tested.
