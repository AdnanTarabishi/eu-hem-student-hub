Where do these experiences come from?

Our first collection links to interviews and testimonials published by EU-HEM partner universities. Each card identifies its publisher. Direct student contributions will appear after the author and an editor have approved the version for publication.

Can I share if I haven’t graduated yet?

Yes. Current students can contribute an experience from a city, semester or part of the programme they have already started. Completed, ongoing and planned stages are shown separately.

Do I have to publish my name?

No. You can choose a first name, a display name or no name. You will preview the details before submission. A small cohort may still recognise a person from the context, so you can choose which optional background details to include.

Can I update or withdraw my experience?

Yes. Keep your submission reference and contact the Student Hub to request a change or withdrawal. Publication changes are reviewed and applied by the editor. You do not need to wait until graduation to share an experience.
