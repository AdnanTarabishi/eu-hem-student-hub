Planned visibility

Shared with the audience you choose.

The planned Gallery will offer two audiences. Here, “private” means visible to verified EU-HEM members.

Planned option

Public

Anyone visiting the Hub can view approved public photos. Photographers and identifiable people must agree to that public audience before publication.

No public photos are available in this preview.

Planned option · Private

Members only

Only verified EU-HEM members can view approved member-only photos. This option requires verified sign-in and protected photo storage before it can open.

Member access is not active in this preview.

Privacy needs real access protection.

Member-only originals, thumbnails and downloads will require verified access. A “private” label or a hidden album cannot protect a publicly accessible photo. This preview stores and displays no private photos.

Consent before sharing

Contributions need the photographer's permission and the consent of identifiable people for the selected audience.

Permission before a wider audience

A member-only photo will require renewed permission before being changed to public visibility.

A clear removal route

Photographers and people shown will be able to request corrections or removal, with instructions alongside published albums.

Future contributions

Your photographs. Your context. Your choice.

Photo contributions will open only after the review process and the relevant storage protections are ready.

  1. Choose your own photograph.Contribute work you took yourself and have permission to share. Avoid images containing documents or unrelated personal information.
  2. Add useful context.Include a caption, preferred photographer credit, city, date and related event where applicable, plus a short accessible image description.
  3. Select the audience.Choose public or verified-members-only visibility and confirm the permission of identifiable people for that audience.
  4. Review before publication.The team will check permissions, captions and the selected audience before any image becomes visible.

Uploads are not open yet.

There is no photo upload or member sign-in on this page. You can suggest album ideas through Contact; please do not send private photographs as part of a suggestion.
