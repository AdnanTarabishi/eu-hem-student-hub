Advanced privacy settings

Choose who may see each detail. Your profile choice above sets the limits; your email is never public. A detail you change here is never made more visible automatically, even if you later change your profile setting.

Anonymous community statistics *

Allow my non-contact profile information to contribute to anonymous, aggregated EU-HEM community statistics?

This may include information such as country represented, academic background, cohort and EU-HEM track. Your name, photo, email and individual profile will never be shown as part of these statistics.

Mobility statistics optional

No individual citizenship or visa response will be published. Small groups may be combined or omitted. This is separate from the community statistics choice above; saying no does not affect your registration or your profile.

Your registration will not be added to the Student Directory. The Student Directory is for current EU-HEM students and alumni. Your information stays private with the Student Hub team, who use it to confirm your connection to EU-HEM.
