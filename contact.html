    Four ways to make the Hub better

    Choose a starting point.

    Each option opens an email with a suggested subject. Add the details that matter, then send when you're ready.

    01

    Flag a correction

    A date looks wrong, a link is broken, or something isn't working as expected.

    Useful to include

    • The page link and what needs changing.
    • An official source, or what happened on your device.
    Report a correction
    02

    Share an idea

    A useful tool, a clearer explanation, or a small change that would make a difference.

    Useful to include

    • What you're trying to do and where you get stuck.
    • How your idea would help you or other students.
    Suggest an improvement
    03

    Privacy or removal

    A question about your information, or something involving you that should be changed or removed.

    Useful to include

    • A link to the page or item concerned.
    • The change or removal you are requesting.
    Send a privacy request
    04

    Contribute something

    Offer original notes, a practical tip, or a resource that other students could find useful.

    Useful to include

    • What you've created and where it belongs.
    • Permission to share it and your preferred credit.
    Offer a contribution

    Include only the details needed to understand your request. If you attach a screenshot, cover unrelated personal information first. Read how messages are handled in the Privacy notice.
