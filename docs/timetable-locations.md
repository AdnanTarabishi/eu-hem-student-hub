# Timetable classroom guide

The Timetable keeps the complete location string from the live University of Bologna feed. `timetable-locations.js` adds practical guidance only when an official UniBo page confirms the building, floor or special entrance.

Checked: **10 October 2026**.

## Main sources

- EU-HEM lecture halls and laboratories: https://corsi.unibo.it/2cycle/euHealthEconomicsManagement/classrooms-labs-and-libraries
- Current 2026/27 UniBo course-timetable pages for Fundamentals in Health Economics, both Statistics options, Our Right to Health, Fundamentals in Healthcare Management, Health Systems, Fundamentals of Econometrics and International Law and Health.

Covered examples include Piazza Scaravilli room floors; the Via Belmeloro 8 entrance for Istologia Carinci; Via Selmi 3 floor 2 for Aula Magna Anatomia Comparata; Via Irnerio 48 Anatomy/Pharmacology rooms; Ranzani 1 versus Ranzani 14; Porta San Donato 1/5; Centotrecento 18; Berti Pichat 5 versus 6–6/2; San Giacomo 3 versus 12; Via Zamboni 32; Via Filippo Re 10; Via Belle Arti 41; and Via San Petronio Vecchio 32.

The guide never invents a floor for an unknown room. Unknown locations keep the full official timetable string and tell students to check UniBo. The live official timetable remains authoritative for room changes.
