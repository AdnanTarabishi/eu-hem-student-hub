## Editorial boundaries

The three reform waves are an organising framework, not three isolated eras.
1987 is the proposal date and 2006 the reform implementation in the deck.
The 50/45/5 financing diagram is not a bill-level copayment split. Care allowance
and risk equalisation have different recipients. The household allowance amount
and prevalence are preserved as slide statements, not independently verified
personal entitlement parameters or current population estimates.

The in-kind/reimbursement distinction is retained. The court statements are kept
within their waiting-time context, not converted to a guarantee of winning or an
assertion that no legal claim is possible. The 70–80% non-contracted range is not
made a universal rate or applied blindly to the foreign invoice. The urgent-care
exception retains the Dutch-cost ceiling from the later cross-border slides.

Page 38 is a compressed account, not an exhaustive list of lawful limitations.
The guide does not claim all inpatient treatment can be refused for financial
reasons. It makes no individual coverage decision and infers no right from
citizenship alone. The source’s spelling of a case name is not used as a recall
question. Repeated slides are synthesised, not copied.

## Separately labelled official checks

Checked 31 October 2026. These are supplementary checks, not statements attributed
to the lecturer and not a claim to extend the assessed syllabus.

1. NZa, Kerncijfers zorgverzekeraars, policy-type table 2024–2026:
   https://www.nza.nl/zorgsectoren/zorgverzekeraars/kerncijfers-zorgverzekeraars
   Lists zero pure reimbursement policies in both 2025 and 2026. The conceptual
   comparison should not be sold as a current product catalogue.
2. European Commission, European Health Insurance Card:
   https://employment-social-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies-and-activities/moving-working-europe/eu-social-security-coordination/european-health-insurance-card_en
   Describes medically necessary care during temporary stays rather than only
   emergencies, with host-system conditions and costs, and excludes travel expressly
   for treatment. No individual eligibility inference is made.
