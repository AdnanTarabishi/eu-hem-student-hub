## 2. Before you move

Semester 2 in Oslo is the spring semester, Semester 3 the autumn semester. The deadlines below come from UiO and SiO; check the exact start date of teaching with EU-HEM. The UiO spring 2027 start date is **not verified**: check the [UiO programme page](https://www.uio.no/english/studies/programmes/hem-master/structure/).

### 3–4 months before
- **Housing:** apply to SiO before the priority deadline: **1 November** for a spring start, **1 June** for an autumn start [S10]. Earlier applicants get offers first [S12].
- **Non-EU students:** start the study permit. You need to show you can pay for your stay, usually with a deposit to UiO [S2][S4]. For spring 2027 the amount is **NOK 92,928**, and the deposit deadline is **1 November**; for autumn the deadline is **15 June** (autumn 2026 amount: NOK 77,440) [S4]. Exchange students may have different rules [S4].
- **Ask EU-HEM** whether UiO treats you as an exchange student or a master's student. It changes your housing priority and your permit paperwork [S4][S11].

### 1–2 months before
- **Non-EU students:** UDI wants all documents by **1 December** (spring start) or **1 July** (autumn start) [S4].
- **EU/EEA students:** register in the UDI portal and book a police appointment (see section 3) [S5].
- **Insurance:** EU/EEA students bring the EHIC (European Health Insurance Card) or private insurance; travel insurance is recommended for everyone [S8].
- **Semester fee:** pay it by **1 February** (spring) or **1 September** (autumn). It includes NOK 690 to SiO, the student welfare organisation [S40]. You need it for SiO housing, the student doctor and the student gym price [S9][S12][S37].
- **Medicines:** bring what you need. Some medicines classed as narcotics need permission for more than 30 days' supply; apply 2–3 months ahead [S9].
- **Money:** a Norwegian bank account is nearly impossible for stays under 6 months, so bring a bank card from home that works abroad [S23].

### First two weeks
- Go to your **police appointment** (EU/EEA and non-EU) [S5][S6].
- Collect your **UiO key card** at the UiO Card Centre in Kristian Ottosens hus, Blindern [S28].
- Buy a **Ruter** 30-day ticket in the Ruter app; carry proof that you are a student this semester [S16].
- Move into SiO housing: rent is paid in advance on the 1st of the month, and you bring your own Wi-Fi router [S14].

## 3. Residence and registration

### EU/EEA students
- You do **not** need a residence permit, but you must register with the police [S3].
- Register in the UDI portal (municipality Oslo, choose the Service Centre for Foreign Workers, SUA), then book a police appointment **within 3 months** of arrival. Book the first free slot, even if it is later [S5].
- Bring: passport or national ID card, EHIC or private insurance, your admission letter from Studentweb and a declaration that you have enough money. Documents must be in English or Norwegian [S5].
- **ID number:** EU/EEA students staying **more than 6 months** register in the National Registry at SUA (passport, rental contract, admission letter) [S7]. Shorter stays get a temporary **D-number**. You need one of these numbers for a bank account, BankID and Vipps [S7][S23][S24].
- Fees and processing time: **not verified**. Check the official page: [UDI, EU/EEA nationals](https://www.udi.no/en/want-to-apply/residence-as-eueea-national/).

### Non-EU students
- You need a **study permit** from UDI (the Norwegian immigration authority) **before you travel**. Apply online or through an embassy, consulate or VFS [S2].
- Usual documents: admission letter and study plan, tuition confirmation, passport, rental contract and the deposit receipt [S2]. Deposit amounts and deadlines: see section 2 [S4].
- **After arrival:** book an appointment with Oslo Police District, Department of Immigration, Schweigaards gate 15 B (phone 22 34 21 00) through the UDI portal. Bring your passport, your address on paper and the SiO deposit receipt. Appointments are limited, so take the first one available [S6].
- **ID number:** the police tell the Tax Administration when your permit is processed. One-semester stays do not get a national ID number; you get a D-number [S7].
- Application fee and processing time: **not verified** (UDI's website could not be checked). Check the official page: [UDI, studies](https://www.udi.no/en/want-to-apply/studies/studying-at-a-university-or-university-college/).

## 4. Healthcare

### EU/EEA students
- Bring your **EHIC** or private insurance. Travel insurance is recommended [S8].
- Whether EU/EEA students get a regular doctor (*fastlege*): **not verified**. Check the official page: [Helsenorge, foreigners in Norway](https://www.helsenorge.no/en/foreigners-in-norway/).

### Non-EU students
- **Stays of 3–12 months:** you may apply to NAV for voluntary membership of the National Insurance Scheme (admission letter, passport, residence card and ID number). Stays over 12 months are members automatically; stays under 3 months cannot join [S8].
- Membership covers Norway only. Private or travel insurance is recommended [S8].

### For all students
- **SiO Health** offers a student doctor (GP), dental care, and mental health and counselling services to students who paid the semester fee. A GP visit has a fee [S9]. It is on the 2nd floor of Kristian Ottosens hus, Blindern [S28].
- **Emergency:** 113 (medical emergency). **Out-of-hours doctor (Legevakten):** 116 117 [S39].

## 5. Housing

- **SiO** is the official student housing organisation. To apply you must be enrolled at an institution linked to SiO, pay the semester fee and be **34 or younger** [S12].
- **Who gets priority:** there is no guarantee for everyone. Exchange students and international master's students who apply for single housing before the deadline are prioritised [S10][S11]. Deadlines: **1 November** (spring start), **1 June** (autumn start) [S10].
- **How:** apply on bolig.sio.no with up to 6 wishes. You can accept "similar housing units" to get an offer sooner, but turning down a similar offer loses your place in the queue [S12].
- **What it costs (SiO, checked 5 October 2026)** [S13]:

| Type | Rent per month |
|---|---|
| Single room, shared kitchen | NOK 4,510–8,333 (most NOK 5,500–7,500) [S13] |
| Studio or flat | NOK 7,228–13,051 (most NOK 7,500–10,000) [S13] |

- **Where:** the big SiO student villages are Kringsjå, Sogn and Bjølsen [S12].
- **Private rentals:** a deposit is usually 3 months' rent, at most 6 months', and it must go into a special deposit account. Rent is paid monthly in advance. Use the standard contract from the Consumer Council (Forbrukerrådet) [S15].
- **Scams:** never pay a deposit in cash or to a personal account [S15]. Which private rental websites to use: **not verified**. Check the official page: [Oslo kommune, renting a home](https://www.oslo.kommune.no/english/find-a-home/renting-a-home/).
- **Help with a landlord dispute:** Forbrukerrådet, Husleietvistutvalget (the rent disputes tribunal) and Leieboerforeningen (the tenants' association) [S15].

## 6. Getting around

- **Ruter** runs the buses, trams, metro and local ferries [S16].
- **Student discount: only for students under 30.** They get 40% off the 30-day ticket: **NOK 393** for zone 1 [S16]. Buy it in the Ruter app and carry proof of student status for the current semester [S16].
- **Without the discount (30 or older):** the adult 30-day ticket for zone 1 is **NOK 655** since 3 May 2026 (was NOK 805). This lower price depends on a state subsidy planned until April 2027, so it may go up again [S18]. A single ticket for zone 1 costs **NOK 46** and is valid for 60 minutes [S17].
- **Airport (Oslo Gardermoen):** the Flytoget airport train costs **NOK 276**, or **NOK 138** with the student discount. This discount has **no age limit**; show a valid student ID [S19][S20].
- **Trains to other cities (Vy):** students **under 30** get 50% off single tickets and 30-day tickets. Foreign students qualify with a valid student ID [S21].
- **City bikes (Oslo Bysykkel):** **NOK 179** for 30 days or **NOK 599** for a year, with 60 minutes per trip included. They run all year, with fewer bikes (studded tyres) in winter [S22].

## 7. Money and phone

- **Bank account:** nearly impossible for stays under 6 months. For 6 months or more you need a Norwegian ID number or D-number and a meeting at the bank; expect it to take several weeks [S23].
- **Vipps** is the common payment app. It needs BankID, a Norwegian ID number or D-number, a Norwegian phone number, an address in the population register and a Norwegian bank account and card [S24]. For a one-semester stay, plan to pay by card instead.
- **Mobile plans (checked 5 October 2026):**
  - Ice: from **NOK 199** a month (5 GB) to NOK 449 [S25]
  - Telia, under 30: **NOK 349** (10 GB) or **NOK 449** (unlimited data) a month [S26]
- If you keep your home SIM card, ask your provider about roaming in Norway (an EEA country) before you go.

## 9. Study places and campus

- **Where EU-HEM teaching happens:** **not verified**. The Institute of Health and Society (HELSAM) works from several buildings, including Frederik Holsts hus, Harald Schelderups hus and Forskningsveien 2B [S30]. Check your timetable or ask EU-HEM.
- **Campuses:** Blindern is the main campus; UiO also uses Gaustad and Domus Medica [S28].
- **Libraries (with your UiO card):**
  - Humanities and Social Sciences Library, Georg Sverdrups hus (Blindern): Mon–Fri 8–22, Sat–Sun 10–18 [S29]
  - Science Library, Vilhelm Bjerknes' hus (Blindern): card access Mon–Fri 8–22, Sat–Sun 8–18 [S29]
  - Life Science Library (Gaustadbekkdalen): card access every day 5–24 [S29]
  - Medical Library at Rikshospitalet (Oslo University Hospital) [S29]
- **UiO key card:** at the UiO Card Centre, Kristian Ottosens hus (building BL02), Blindern [S28].

## 10. Food and daily life

- **Canteens:** SiO runs the cafeterias on campus, with student-friendly prices and vegetarian and halal options [S31]. Example: the vegetarian "Grønn buffet" is priced by weight, **NOK 17.50 per 100 g** (normal price) [S32].
- **Supermarkets, cheapest first:** REMA 1000 and KIWI are the cheap chains; MENY and Joker are dearer. This order comes from an independent budget-travel blog, so treat it as a rough guide [S34].
- **Sundays:** most grocery shops are **closed on Sundays and public holidays**. Some kiosks, small independent shops and a few 24-hour petrol stations stay open [S33].
- **Second-hand and saving money:** Finn.no, Tise, Facebook Marketplace, flea markets, Fretex, and Too Good To Go for leftover food [S33].

## 11. Weather and what to pack

- Average temperature at Blindern: about **−2.3 °C in January** and **17.7 °C in July**. About 837 mm of rain and snow a year [S36].
- The climate is milder than the latitude suggests. Summer often goes above 20 °C, and winters are cold enough for skiing; in summer the sky never gets fully dark [S35].
- **Pack for a spring semester (January–June):** a warm winter coat, hat, gloves, warm layers and waterproof shoes with good grip for snow and ice. Bring a light rain jacket for spring.

## 12. Sport, social life and student organisations

- **SiO Athletica** (student gyms): **NOK 270** a month with a 12-month commitment or **NOK 310** without, for UiO students who paid the semester fee. Without the semester fee: NOK 535 / NOK 555 [S37].
- **ESN Oslo** (Erasmus Student Network) organises activities for international and Norwegian students and is open to new members: oslo.esn.no [S38].
- **Outdoors:** skiing and snowboarding at Tryvann, hundreds of kilometres of cross-country tracks in Nordmarka (many start at Holmenkollen), and island trips in the Oslo Fjord by boat [S35].
- **Student associations:** see UiO's [list of student associations](https://www.uio.no/english/student-life/associations/).
