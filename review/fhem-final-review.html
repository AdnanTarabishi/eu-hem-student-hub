01 · KNOW THE PAPER

Score the exam you actually have.

The practice papers use a 6 + 2 + 1 structure. Allocate time before you begin and treat the open question as the centre of the paper.

MCQ

Look for “most complete.”

A partly correct option earns 2, but the fully correct option usually combines the mechanism with its implication.

Binary

Attack absolute words.

“Always”, “proves”, “necessarily” and concept reversals are common traps. State the assumption before choosing.

Open

Write causal chains.

Do not list theories. Diagnose the institution, explain behaviour, compare reforms, recommend and monitor.

04 · TA SESSION 22 OCTOBER

Grossman in five pictures—and the two form questions.

The TA file adds a compact way to reason: identify stock, flow and hurdle; then ask whether a change moves the return curve or the cost line.

1

The machine

Health stock H wears out at δ and is repaired by gross investment I.

Hₜ = (1−δ)Hₜ₋₁ + Iₜ₋₁
2

What it makes

Health produces healthy time for work, home production and life—not healthcare itself.

3

The frontier

Below C, health and the home good rise together. Beyond C, extra health costs other consumption.

4

The rule

Invest while the marginal return exceeds r + δ; stop at H*.

5

Curve or line?

Wage, education and care price move returns. Age and interest move the hurdle.

FORM QUESTION · CHAPTER 4

δ or γ? Identify by role, not by letter.

A depreciation rate sits in the health-stock constraint and in r + δ. A discount rate sits in preferences and weights future utility. Allostatic load raises biological depreciation; Fuchs uses impatience as a common cause of lower schooling and health investment.

FORM QUESTION · CHAPTER 15

A physician fee cut has two opposing effects.

Substitution: leisure becomes cheaper in forgone income, so work falls. Income: the physician is poorer; if leisure is normal, leisure falls and work rises. More services after a fee cut are therefore not proof of induced demand.
