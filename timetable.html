How is this calculated?

Day, Week and Month use finished scheduled hours ÷ all published scheduled hours in the selected period × 100. List uses completed published classes ÷ all published matching classes × 100, so it shows overall lecture completion across the full published schedule. Weeks run Monday–Sunday. A class counts as complete only after its scheduled end in Bologna time. Course, search and My courses only filters apply; Show teacher and Show past classes do not affect the percentage.

This is scheduled teaching progress, not attendance, learning progress or credits earned. It excludes personal study and exams. Unpublished classes are unknown, and the total can change when UniBo changes its timetable. List view shows overall progress across all published matching classes, including finished classes hidden from the agenda. Save your study plan and use My courses only for a personalised overall percentage.
