      How to subscribe

      Google Calendar (Android, computer)
      1. Tap Google Calendar above. On a phone, do this once on a computer: Google only adds subscriptions on the web.
      2. Confirm Add. The calendar appears under "Other calendars".
      3. Google refreshes subscriptions slowly (sometimes once a day).
      Apple Calendar (iPhone, Mac)
      1. Tap Apple Calendar above.
      2. Confirm Subscribe, then Add.
      3. On iPhone you can choose how often it refreshes in Settings → Calendar → Accounts → Subscribed Calendars.
      Outlook (outlook.com, Office 365)
      1. Tap Outlook above and sign in.
      2. Check the name and choose Import.

      Exam end times are estimates (2 hours). If you change your study plan, subscribe to the new link and remove the old calendar.
