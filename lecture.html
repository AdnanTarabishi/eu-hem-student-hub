Exercise 5.1, without replacement

Population: 65, 89, 75, 64, 86 kg. Every sample of a given size is equally likely. Here we enumerate all combinations, ignoring order.

Notice: the expected sample mean stays at 75.8 kg. The spread decreases, reaching zero when everyone is measured.
